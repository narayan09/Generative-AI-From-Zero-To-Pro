# Jira FAQ

## What is an Epic in Jira?
An Epic is a large body of work that can be broken down into multiple smaller tasks (called Stories or Tasks). Epics are used to organise work around a common goal, theme, or feature. In Jira, Epics have a special issue type and can span multiple sprints.

## How do I create a Sprint?
To create a Sprint:
1. Go to your Scrum board.
2. Click the "Backlog" tab.
3. Click "Create Sprint" at the top of the backlog.
4. Drag and drop issues from the backlog into the Sprint.
5. Click "Start Sprint" and set the duration.

## What is the difference between a Bug and a Task?
A Bug represents a defect or flaw in the product, while a Task is a unit of work that needs to be completed (e.g., implementation, documentation). Bugs are often prioritised separately and tracked differently.

## How to assign an issue to a user?
Open the issue, click the "Assignee" field (usually top-right), and select a user from the drop-down list. You can also type a name to search. If the user is not in the list, they may not have permission to be assigned in that project.

## What are the default priority levels?
Jira provides five default priority levels:
- Blocker
- Critical
- Major
- Minor
- Trivial
These can be customised by administrators.

## How do I search for all issues assigned to me?
Use the JQL (Jira Query Language) query: `assignee = currentUser()`. You can also use the "My open issues" gadget on your dashboard.

## What is a Jira workflow?
A workflow represents the lifecycle of an issue. It consists of statuses (e.g., Open, In Progress, Done) and transitions between them. Each project can have a custom workflow.

## How can I add a comment to an issue?
Open the issue, scroll to the "Activity" section, click "Comment", type your text, and click "Save". You can also use `@mentions` to notify other users.

## What is a Component in Jira?
A Component is a sub‑section of a project used to group issues related to a specific module, feature, or team. Components can have a default assignee.
